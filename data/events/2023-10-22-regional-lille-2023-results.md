# Regional Championship Lille 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Lille Regional Championship (France) had 855 Masters players. Konsta Kallama won with Mew VMAX / Genesect V / Meloetta. Limitless TCG lists the event as starting on 21 October 2023; the Serebii calendar gives 22-23 October.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Battle Styles to Obsidian Flames, before Paradox Rift was released.
- **Top 8:** 1st Konsta Kallama (Mew / Genesect / Meloetta), 2nd Arthur Trutzschler (Comfey / Sableye), 3rd Owyn Kamerman (Chien-Pao ex / Baxcalibur), 4th Patrik Bartosovic (Lugia / Archeops), 5th Alberto Conti (Giratina VSTAR / Comfey), 6th Gabor Van Meenen (Mew / Genesect), 7th Hermanni Hietalahti (Hisuian Goodra / Comfey), 8th Fabien Pujol (Gardevoir ex).
- About ten Gardevoir ex decks appear in the published standings. The two highest, 8th (Fabien Pujol) and 12th (Edwyn Mesman), played no Avery (Chilling Reign).
