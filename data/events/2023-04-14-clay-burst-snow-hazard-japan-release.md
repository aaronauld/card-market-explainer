# Japanese Clay Burst and Snow Hazard released

## Summary

Clay Burst (SV2D) and Snow Hazard (SV2P), the second pair of Japanese Scarlet & Violet expansions, were released in Japan on 14 April 2023. Their featured Pokémon are Ting-Lu ex (Clay Burst) and Chien-Pao ex (Snow Hazard).

## Details

- **Clay Burst** includes Ting-Lu ex and the Supporter Iono. **Snow Hazard** includes Chien-Pao ex, Baxcalibur and Super Rod.
- Each set has 71 cards in its main numbering (99 with secret rares).
- These were Japanese releases only. Ting-Lu ex, Chien-Pao ex and Iono were released in English in Paldea Evolved on 9 June 2023.
- The release fell on the same day the 2023 Standard rotation took effect for in-person English events (14 April 2023), but the two are separate events.
