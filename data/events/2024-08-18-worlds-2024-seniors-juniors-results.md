# Pokémon TCG World Championships 2024, Seniors and Juniors winners

## Summary

At the 2024 Pokémon TCG World Championships in Honolulu, Hawaii (16-18 August 2024), Evan Pavelski won the Senior Division with a Regidrago VSTAR deck, and Sakuya Ota won the Junior Division with an Ancient Box deck.

## Details

- **Senior Division champion:** Evan Pavelski, playing Regidrago VSTAR (Silver Tempest 136).
- **Junior Division champion:** Sakuya Ota, playing Ancient Box.
- Seniors and Juniors are the two age divisions below Masters. The Masters winner was Fernando Cifuentes with Iron Thorns ex.

## Sources

Division winners and decks from PokeGuardian's report on the 2024 World Championship Decks (19 December 2024).
