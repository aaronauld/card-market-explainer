# Regional Championship Knoxville, Tennessee 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Knoxville, Tennessee Regional Championship ran from 24 to 26 February 2023, with 1,139 Masters players. Andrew Hedrick won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops, his second Regional win of the month after Orlando.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Andrew Hedrick (Lugia Archeops), 2nd Brandon Salazar (Eternatus VMAX / Galarian Weezing), 3rd Rahul Reddy (Regigigas), 4th Nicholas Moffitt (Comfey / Rayquaza), 5th Piper Lepine (Origin Forme Palkia / Inteleon), 6th Grant Shen (Arceus / Duraludon VMAX), 7th Lance Bradshaw (Lugia Archeops), 8th Alex Schemanske (Vikavolt).
- **Format:** Standard, with Silver Tempest as the newest legal set. The top 8 was more varied than at Orlando, with only two Lugia decks.
- The event was held before the 2023 Standard rotation and before the Scarlet & Violet base set was legal in English events.
