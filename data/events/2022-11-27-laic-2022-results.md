# Latin America International Championships 2022-23, Masters results

## Summary

The Latin America International Championships for the 2023 season ran in São Paulo, Brazil, from 25 to 27 November 2022, with 976 Masters players. Tord Reklev of Norway won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Tord Reklev (Lugia Archeops), 2nd Lucas Calzà (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Grant Manley (Lost Zone Box), 4th Jon Eng (Lugia Archeops), 5th Sander Wojcik (Yveltal Control), 6th Pedro Pertusi (Lugia Archeops), 7th Ciaran Farah (Lugia Archeops), 8th Agustin Campo (Lugia Archeops).
- Lugia VSTAR / Archeops, built around cards from Silver Tempest, took six of the eight top spots.
