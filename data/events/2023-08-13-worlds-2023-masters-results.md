# Pokémon TCG World Championships 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The 2023 Pokémon TCG World Championships ran at Pacifico Yokohama, Japan, from 11 to 13 August 2023, the first Worlds held in Japan. Vance Kelley of the United States won the Masters Division with Mew VMAX (a Fusion Strike deck), beating Tord Reklev's Gardevoir ex in the final.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Vance Kelley (Fusion Mew), 2nd Tord Reklev (Gardevoir), 3rd Michael Pramawat (Giratina Lost Zone Box), 4th Azul Garcia Griego (Fusion Mew), 5th Dionsius Lee (Fusion Mew), 6th Victor Manuel Garcia Montes (Gardevoir), 7th Shoichi Saito (Lost Zone Box), 8th Pang Kai Hing (Giratina Lost Zone Box).
- Mew VMAX decks took three of the eight top spots.
