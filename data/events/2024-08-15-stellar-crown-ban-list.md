# Stellar Crown banned list announcement (Duskull banned in Expanded)

## Summary

On 15 August 2024, ahead of the Stellar Crown release, The Pokémon Company International announced that Duskull (Cosmic Eclipse 83) is banned in the Expanded format. Nothing was banned in Standard.

## Details

- **Banned in Expanded:** Duskull (Cosmic Eclipse 83). The reason given was that Duskull, together with Dusclops, allowed decks to win on the first turn.
- **Standard:** no changes.
