# Regional Championship Portland, Oregon 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Portland, Oregon Regional Championship started on 6 May 2023, with 1,088 Masters players. Landen Kaetler of Canada won with Arceus VSTAR / Origin Forme Giratina VSTAR.

## Details

- **Format:** Battle Styles to Scarlet & Violet.
- **Top 8:** 1st Landen Kaetler (Arceus / Giratina), 2nd Regan Retzloff (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Grant Manley (Lost Zone Box, Comfey / Sableye), 4th Andrew Gantner (Lost Zone Box), 5th Alex Schemanske (Lost Zone Box), 6th Austin Drake (Mew / Genesect), 7th Anthony Perez (Miraidon / Flaaffy), 8th Nathan Stratford (Arceus / Duraludon / Umbreon).
- Comfey / Sableye Lost Zone decks were the most common deck in the top 32, with 12 of the 32 places.
- Gardevoir ex decks placed 15th, 25th and 29th.
- One Inteleon VMAX / Rapid Strike Urshifu deck was recorded, finishing 94th.
