# Special Event Bologna 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Bologna Special Event in Italy ran on 1 and 2 June 2024, with 1,008 Masters players. Kim Pobega of Italy won with an Iron Hands ex / Iron Crown ex deck. It finished five days before the North America International Championships began.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Temporal Forces (BRS-TEF). Twilight Masquerade, released on 24 May 2024, was not legal.
- **Top 8:** 1st Kim Pobega (Iron Hands / Iron Crown), 2nd Kristian Hodas (Comfey / Sableye), 3rd Angelo Coralluzzo (Gardevoir), 4th Christian Graziano (Lugia / Archeops), 5th Filippo Colamorea (Pidgeot), 6th Cristian Sarnataro (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 7th Alessandro Bax (Pidgeot), 8th Konstantin Shapkin (Arceus / Giratina).
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift): Andrea Calciolari finished 13th with Roaring Moon ex / Dudunsparce.
- Arceus / Giratina decks placed three players in the top 16 (8th, 10th, 16th).
- No Regidrago VSTAR deck finished in the top 16.
