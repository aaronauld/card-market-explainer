# Scarlet & Violet—Stellar Crown released

## Summary

Stellar Crown, the seventh main expansion of the Scarlet & Violet series, was released in English on 13 September 2024, with more than 170 cards. Terapagos is the headline Pokémon.

## Details

- **Featured Pokémon:** Terapagos, plus Cinderace ex, Lapras ex and Galvantula ex as Stellar Tera Pokémon ex, and Hydrapple ex.
- **Other contents:** new ACE SPEC cards, over 20 Trainer cards, and more than 30 cards with special illustrations.
- It is the English counterpart of the Japanese set Stellar Miracle (19 July 2024).
- The set was not yet legal at Regional Baltimore (13-15 September 2024), which used Brilliant Stars to Shrouded Fable. The Dortmund and Joinville Regionals on 28-29 September used Brilliant Stars to Stellar Crown.
