# Regional Championship Stockholm 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Stockholm Regional Championship in Sweden ran on 11 and 12 May 2024, with 675 Masters players. Azul Garcia Griego of the United States won with a Snorlax deck.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Temporal Forces (BRS-TEF). Twilight Masquerade was not yet legal.
- **Top 8:** 1st Azul Garcia Griego (Snorlax), 2nd Hermanni Hietalahti (Comfey / Sableye), 3rd Tord Reklev (Charizard / Pidgeot), 4th Jon Eng (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 5th Brent Tonisson (Comfey / Sableye), 6th Stian Nilsson (Comfey / Sableye / Charizard), 7th Juho Kallama (Iron Hands / Iron Crown), 8th Fares Sekkoum (Charizard / Pidgeot).
- Comfey / Sableye decks took three of the top eight places. Charizard / Pidgeot placed four players in the top 16 (3rd, 8th, 10th, 11th).
- No Regidrago VSTAR or Roaring Moon ex deck finished in the top 16.
