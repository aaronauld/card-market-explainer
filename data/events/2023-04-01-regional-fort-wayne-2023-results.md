# Regional Championship Fort Wayne, Indiana 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Fort Wayne, Indiana Regional Championship started on 1 April 2023, with 1,154 Masters players. Maxwell Johnson won with a Goodra VSTAR / Lost Zone Box deck.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Maxwell Johnson (Goodra Lost Zone Box), 2nd Noah Sawyer (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Justin Stites (Lugia Archeops), 4th Zander Bennett (Mew / Genesect), 5th Grant Manley (Regis), 6th Austin Charles (Goodra Lost Zone Box), 7th Lance Bradshaw (Lugia Archeops), 8th Raymond Long (Mew / Genesect).
- The event was played in the format before the Scarlet & Violet base set became tournament legal.
