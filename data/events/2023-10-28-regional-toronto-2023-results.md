# Regional Championship Toronto 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Toronto Regional Championship started on 28 October 2023, with 1,543 Masters players. JW Kriewall won with Miraidon ex / Flaaffy.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st JW Kriewall (Miraidon Flaaffy), 2nd Demi Eaton (Gardevoir), 3rd Azul Garcia Griego (Charizard / Pidgeot), 4th Christian Moreno (Lugia Archeops), 5th Hermanni Hietalahti (Goodra Lost Zone Box), 6th Jake Riggs (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 7th Grant Shen (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 8th Dylan Gunn (Lost Zone Box).
- About 20 Gardevoir ex lists were published from the event.
- It was played before Paradox Rift (3 November 2023) was released.
