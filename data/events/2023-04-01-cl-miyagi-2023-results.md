# Champions League Miyagi 2023 (Japan), results

## Summary

Champions League Miyagi, a large Japanese tournament with 2,488 players, was held on 1 April 2023. Shin Inageta won with a Miraidon ex / Regieleki VMAX deck.

## Details

- **Winning list (Shin Inageta, Miraidon / Regieleki):** 3 Miraidon ex, 3 Regieleki V, 3 Regieleki VMAX and 2 Exp. Share, among other cards. Miraidon ex comes from the Scarlet & Violet base set; Regieleki V and VMAX are from Silver Tempest.
- **Top 8:** 1st Shin Inageta (Miraidon Regieleki), 2nd Shinya Kumagai (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Naoto Iwasaki (Arceus Giratina), 4th Ryuto Hadano (Lugia Archeops), 5th Hibiki Emura (Lost Zone Box), 6th Shizuya Yamamoto (Lost Zone Box), 7th Souichirou Anamizu (Lost Zone Box), 8th Yushi Okazaki (Lugia Archeops).
- **Gardevoir ex:** Taiga Kayama finished 10th with Gardevoir ex, playing 2 Gardevoir (Chilling Reign 61).
- At the previous Champions League (Aichi, 25 February 2023), no Miraidon deck finished in the top 16.
- Japanese tournaments use Japanese card releases, which come out before the matching English sets.
