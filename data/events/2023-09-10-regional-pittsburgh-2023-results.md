# Regional Championship Pittsburgh 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Pittsburgh Regional Championship (Pennsylvania, USA) was part of the 2024 Championship Series. Masters play began on 9 September 2023 with 1,778 players, and Andrew Estrada won with Comfey / Sableye (Lost Zone Box).

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Battle Styles to Obsidian Flames. Scarlet & Violet—151 and Paradox Rift were not yet legal.
- **Top 8:** 1st Andrew Estrada (Comfey / Sableye), 2nd Jesse Parker (Miraidon ex / Flaaffy), 3rd Collin Merli-Matthews (Mew / Genesect / Meloetta), 4th Andrew Hedrick (Lugia VSTAR / Archeops), 5th Michael Chen (Giratina VSTAR / Comfey), 6th Adam Reinhardt (Miraidon ex / Flaaffy), 7th Lucas Xing (Chien-Pao ex / Baxcalibur), 8th Rahul Reddy (Lugia VSTAR / Archeops).
- Gardevoir ex did not reach the top 8; the best Gardevoir finishes in the standings were 11th (Michael Davidson) and 30th (Tord Reklev).
- Lugia / Archeops was the most common archetype among the published decklists.
