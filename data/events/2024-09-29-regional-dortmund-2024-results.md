# Regional Championship Dortmund 2024 (September), Masters results

## Summary

The Dortmund Regional Championship in Germany ran on 28 and 29 September 2024 at the Westfalenhallen, with 1,815 Masters players. Ryuki Okada of Japan won with a Dragapult ex / Dusknoir deck. It was one of the first major events with Stellar Crown legal.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Stellar Crown (BRS-SCR).
- **Top 8:** 1st Ryuki Okada (Dragapult / Dusknoir), 2nd Rahul Reddy (Lugia / Archeops), 3rd Jelle van Kampen (Gholdengo), 4th Stéphane Ivanoff (Origin Forme Palkia / Dusknoir), 5th Jan Hausmann (Raging Bolt / Ogerpon), 6th Stephen Law (Iron Thorns), 7th Glenn Bauwens (Miraidon), 8th James de Boer (Lugia / Archeops).
- **Regidrago VSTAR** (Silver Tempest 136) decks: 10th Yoann Barszezak and 15th Jon Eng; none in the top 8.
- **Terapagos ex** (Stellar Crown): Tord Reklev finished 9th with Terapagos / Noctowl.
- Raging Bolt / Ogerpon placed three players in the top 16 (5th, 11th, 13th).
