# Regional Championship Orlando, Florida 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Orlando, Florida Regional Championship ran from 3 to 5 February 2023, with 1,485 players in the Masters division. Andrew Hedrick won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops, and Lugia Archeops took six of the eight top spots.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Andrew Hedrick (Lugia Archeops), 2nd Isaiah Bradner (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Nicholas Moffitt (Comfey / Rayquaza), 4th Calvin Connor (Lugia Archeops), 5th Regan Retzloff (Lugia Archeops), 6th Jon Eng (Lugia Archeops), 7th Austin Reed (Lugia Archeops), 8th Grant Hays (Mew / Genesect).
- **Format:** Standard, with Silver Tempest as the newest legal set. Lugia VSTAR and Archeops both come from Silver Tempest.
- The event was held before the 2023 Standard rotation and before the Scarlet & Violet base set was legal.
