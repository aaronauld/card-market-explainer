# Regional Championship Hartford, Connecticut 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Hartford, Connecticut Regional Championship started on 20 May 2023, with 1,059 Masters players. Rowan Stavenow of Canada won with Mew VMAX / Genesect V / Meloetta.

## Details

- **Format:** Battle Styles to Scarlet & Violet.
- **Top 8:** 1st Rowan Stavenow (Mew / Genesect / Meloetta), 2nd Grant Shen (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Regan Retzloff (Lugia Archeops), 4th Luke Smith (Mew / Genesect), 5th Daniel Altavilla (Lost Zone Box, Comfey / Sableye), 6th Raymond Long (Lost Zone Box), 7th Ryan Harris (Arceus / Duraludon / Umbreon), 8th Shashi Naraine (Mew / Genesect).
- Comfey / Sableye Lost Zone decks took 9 of the top 32 places, and two Hisuian Goodra / Comfey decks also made the top 32 (24th and 32nd).
- Only one Gardevoir ex deck made the top 32 (27th). No Inteleon deck made the top 32.
