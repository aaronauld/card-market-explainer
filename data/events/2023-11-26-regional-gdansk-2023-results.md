# Regional Championship Gdańsk 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Gdańsk Regional Championship (Poland) had 997 Masters players, with Masters play starting on 25 November 2023, the weekend after the Latin America International Championships. Łukasz Mazurkiewicz won with Snorlax (stall). It was one of the first European events with Paradox Rift legal.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Battle Styles to Paradox Rift.
- **Top 8:** 1st Łukasz Mazurkiewicz (Snorlax), 2nd Erin Kingma (Charizard ex / Pidgeot ex), 3rd Luke Kirkham (Origin Forme Palkia VSTAR), 4th Robin Schulz (Charizard ex / Pidgeot ex), 5th Tord Reklev (Comfey / Sableye), 6th Thomas Jones (Charizard ex), 7th Jamie Hindley (Mew / Genesect), 8th Ewan West (Mew / Genesect).
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift 124) decks: eight in the published standings, the best being Maciej Dmoch in 10th.
- **Gardevoir ex** decks: seven in the published standings, the best 21st (Rune Heiremans).
- **Klawf** (Paradox Rift 105): one deck, 68th (Cédric Paporello).
