# Paradise Dragona released in Japan

## Summary

Paradise Dragona, a Japanese Scarlet & Violet expansion focused on Dragon-type Pokémon, was released in Japan on 13 September 2024, the same day Stellar Crown came out in English.

## Details

- **Pokémon ex:** Black Kyurem ex (11), Latias ex (19), Flygon ex (27), Archaludon ex (37), Alolan Exeggutor ex (40) and Cyclizar ex (51), with extra rare printings later in the set.
- **Dragon-type cards** also include Altaria (41), Dialga (42), Palkia (43), Turtonator (44), and Applin, Flapple and Appletun (45-47).
- **Trainers:** Energy Search Pro, Call Bell, Dusk Ball, Meddling Memo, Megaton Blower, Dragon Potion and Counter Gain, and the Supporters Drayton, Surfer, Drasna and Lissia's Appeal.
- It is a Japanese-only release at this date; its cards were not legal in English-language tournaments.
