# Regional Championship Los Angeles 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Los Angeles Regional Championship ran from 24 to 26 May 2024, with 2,073 Masters players. Lucas Xing of Canada won with a Pidgeot deck. Twilight Masquerade was released on the first day of the event but was not legal there.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Temporal Forces (BRS-TEF).
- **Top 8:** 1st Lucas Xing (Pidgeot), 2nd Brandon Baughn (Lugia / Archeops), 3rd Regan Retzloff (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 4th Zane Graham (Lugia / Archeops), 5th Abaan Ahmed (Pidgeot), 6th Neo Erlandsson (Pidgeot), 7th Nico Gist (Iron Hands / Iron Crown), 8th Caleb Rogerson (Snorlax).
- Pidgeot decks took three of the top eight places and five of the top 16 (also 10th Isaiah Bradner and 13th Ian Robb).
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift): one deck in the top 16, Jose Marzan in 16th.
- Origin Forme Dialga VSTAR / Metang finished 11th (Jake Santiago) and 14th (Kevin Sun).
- No Regidrago VSTAR deck finished in the top 16.
