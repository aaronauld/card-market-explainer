# Scarlet & Violet—151 released in English

## Summary

Scarlet & Violet—151, a special set built around the original 151 Kanto Pokémon, was released in English on 22 September 2023.

## Details

- **Size:** 165 numbered cards and 207 cards including secret rares (TCGdex). The original Pokémon appear in Pokédex order.
- **Pokémon ex:** include Venusaur ex, Charizard ex, Blastoise ex, Alakazam ex, Zapdos ex, Kangaskhan ex, Jynx ex and Mew ex (151 Mew ex is card 151).
- **Products:** announced products included an Elite Trainer Box, a Booster Bundle of six packs, a Poster Collection, a Binder Collection, Alakazam ex and Zapdos ex boxes, energy-symbol tins and an Ultra-Premium Collection with Mew ex and Mewtwo promos (the last three on 6 October 2023).
- It was released between Obsidian Flames (11 August 2023) and Paradox Rift (3 November 2023).
