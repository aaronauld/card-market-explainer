# Night Wanderer released in Japan

## Summary

Night Wanderer, a Japanese Scarlet & Violet expansion, was released in Japan on 7 June 2024. Its cards were later released in English in Shrouded Fable (2 August 2024).

## Details

- **Size:** 64 cards in the main numbered set, plus secret rares.
- **Pokémon ex:** Kingdra ex (12), Revavroom ex (15), Okidogi ex (36), Munkidori ex (37), Fezandipiti ex (38) and Pecharunt ex (39), each with extra rare printings later in the set.
- **Kyurem (Night Wanderer 47):** the same Kyurem that came to English as Kyurem (Shrouded Fable 47).
- **Dusknoir:** numbered 20, with a rare printing at 70.
- The release fell on the first day of the North America International Championships (7-9 June 2024). That event used English sets only, so Night Wanderer cards were not legal there.
