# Scarlet & Violet—Obsidian Flames released

## Summary

Obsidian Flames, the third main expansion of the Scarlet & Violet series, was released in English on 11 August 2023, the same weekend the 2023 World Championships began.

## Details

- **Featured Pokémon ex:** Charizard ex, Tyranitar ex, Eiscue ex, Vespiquen ex, Dragonite ex, Greedent ex, Revavroom ex and Melmetal ex.
