# Scarlet & Violet base set released

## Summary

The Scarlet & Violet base set, the first expansion of the Scarlet & Violet series, was released in English on 31 March 2023. It brought back Pokémon ex and introduced Tera Pokémon ex.

## Details

- **Featured Pokémon ex:** the Legendary Pokémon Koraidon ex and Miraidon ex, plus Arcanine ex and Gyarados ex. The set has more than 10 new Pokémon ex and over 190 cards in total.
- **Also in the set:** Gardevoir ex (Scarlet & Violet 86, with alternate printings 228 and 245) and a reprint of the Item card Exp. Share (Scarlet & Violet 174). Miraidon ex is Scarlet & Violet 81 (alternate printings 227, 244, 253).
- **Rules change:** from this set, Pokémon Tools became their own type of Trainer card instead of a kind of Item card, and older Tool cards received errata to match.
- The release came two weeks before the 2023 Standard rotation took effect for in-person events (14 April 2023).
