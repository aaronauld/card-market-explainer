# Ancient Roar and Future Flash (Japanese SV4K / SV4M) released in Japan

## Summary

Ancient Roar (古代の咆哮, SV4K) and Future Flash (未来の一閃, SV4M), a pair of Japanese Scarlet & Violet expansions about Ancient and Future Paradox Pokémon, were released together in Japan on 27 October 2023. Their cards make up most of the English Paradox Rift set (3 November 2023).

## Details

- **Size:** each set has 66 numbered cards, 95 including secret rares.
- **Ancient Roar Pokémon ex:** Roaring Moon ex (card 054, with secret rare printings 084, 090 and 093), Sandy Shocks ex, Armarouge ex, Tsareena ex, Golisopod ex and Cofagrigus ex. It also has the Supporter Professor Sada's Vitality.
- **Future Flash Pokémon ex:** Iron Valiant ex, Iron Hands ex, Toxtricity ex, Aegislash ex, Altaria ex and Maushold ex. It also has Professor Turo's Scenario.
- Japanese sets are legal only in Japanese tournaments.
