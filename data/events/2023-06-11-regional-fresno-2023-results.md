# Regional Championship Fresno, California 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Fresno, California Regional Championship ran on 10-11 June 2023 at the Fresno Convention Center, with 903 Masters players. Regan Retzloff won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops.

## Details

- **Format:** Battle Styles to Scarlet & Violet.
- **Top 8:** 1st Regan Retzloff (Lugia Archeops), 2nd Caleb Gedemer (Lost Zone Box, Comfey / Sableye), 3rd Andrew Hedrick (Mew / Genesect), 4th Calvin Connor (Gardevoir), 5th David Tomhave-Alexie (Darkrai), 6th Kristopher Kemp (Lugia Archeops), 7th Maia Melban (Arceus / Aerodactyl), 8th Le Bui (Mew / Genesect).
- Gedemer's Lost Zone Box list was a faster build that used Drapion V instead of Kyogre, and he went 12-2-4.
- Two Inteleon VMAX / Rapid Strike Urshifu VMAX decks finished just outside the top 16: Peter Buddensiek 17th and Aaron Walker 22nd.
- Gardevoir ex decks took 6 of the top 32 places, and Comfey / Sableye Lost Zone decks took 8.
