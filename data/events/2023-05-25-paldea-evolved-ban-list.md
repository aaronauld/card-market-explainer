# Paldea Evolved banned list and rule changes announcement (no bans)

## Summary

On 25 May 2023, ahead of the Paldea Evolved release, The Pokémon Company International published its banned list and rule changes announcement. No cards were banned in Standard or Expanded.

## Details

- No new bans in either format.
- The announcement included errata (wording changes) for Super Rod and Superior Energy Retrieval.
