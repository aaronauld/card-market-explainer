# Regional Championship Indianapolis 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Indianapolis Regional Championship ran from 3 to 5 May 2024, with 2,342 Masters players. Andrew Hedrick won with an Origin Forme Dialga VSTAR / Metang deck.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Andrew Hedrick (Dialga / Metang), 2nd Grant Shen (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 3rd Dean Nezam (Lost Zone Box), 4th Grant Manley (Pidgeot Control), 5th Nicholas Moffitt (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 6th Eddie North (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 7th Andrew Mahone (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 8th Ian Robb (Charizard / Pidgeot).
- Origin Forme Dialga V comes from Astral Radiance (113, with a full-art printing at 177).
