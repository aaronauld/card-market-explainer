# Scarlet & Violet—Shrouded Fable released

## Summary

Shrouded Fable, a special expansion in the Scarlet & Violet series, was released in English on 2 August 2024, two weeks before the 2024 World Championships.

## Details

- **Featured Pokémon ex:** Pecharunt ex, which leads the Loyal Three, with Revavroom ex and Kingdra ex also highlighted.
