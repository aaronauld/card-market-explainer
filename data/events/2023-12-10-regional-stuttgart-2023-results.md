# Regional Championship Stuttgart 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Stuttgart Regional Championship (Germany) had 982 Masters players, with Masters play starting on 9 December 2023. Christian Fontenot won with Comfey / Sableye / Charizard ex. A Roaring Moon ex deck reached the top 4.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Battle Styles to Paradox Rift.
- **Top 8:** 1st Christian Fontenot (Comfey / Sableye / Charizard ex), 2nd Simon Tschan (Miraidon ex / Flaaffy), 3rd Alberto Conti (Giratina VSTAR / Comfey), 4th Kristian Hodas (Comfey / Roaring Moon ex / Iron Hands ex), 5th Ethan Wilde (Mew / Genesect), 6th Théo Grimoud (Rapid Strike Urshifu VMAX / Inteleon VMAX), 7th Gino Pili (Rapid Strike Urshifu VMAX / Inteleon VMAX), 8th Dustin Bachmann (Charizard ex / Comfey).
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift 124): four decks in the published standings: 4th (Kristian Hodas), 9th (Luigi Vezzoni), 52nd (Hermanni Hietalahti) and 81st (Aleksander Domek).
- **Klawf** (Paradox Rift 105): four decks, the best 45th (Arnaud Bonn).
- **Gardevoir ex**: about 15 decks in the published standings, the best 15th (Bryan de Vries).
