# Shiny Treasure ex (Japanese SV4a high class pack) released in Japan

## Summary

Shiny Treasure ex (シャイニートレジャーex, SV4a), a Japanese high class pack focused on shiny Pokémon, was released in Japan on 1 December 2023.

## Details

- **Product:** 10 cards per pack at a suggested price of 550 yen; 190 numbered cards and over 300 including secret rares (TCGdex).
- **Reprinted Pokémon ex:** Koraidon ex, Miraidon ex, Ting-Lu ex, Chien-Pao ex, Mew ex, Charizard ex and Gardevoir ex.
- **New Pokémon ex:** Great Tusk ex and Iron Treads ex.
- **New Trainers:** Clive, a Technical Machine (ワザマシン かじばのいっぱつ), and Paldean Student (two illustrations).
- Art rares and special art rares feature Palafin, Pawmi, Charizard ex and Gardevoir ex.
- Japanese products are legal only in Japanese tournaments.
