# Europe International Championships 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Europe International Championships (EUIC) 2023 ran at ExCeL London from 14 to 16 April 2023, with 1,525 Masters players. Alex Schemanske of the United States won with Arceus VSTAR / Duraludon VMAX.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Alex Schemanske (Arceus Duraludon), 2nd Tord Reklev (Gardevoir), 3rd Pablo Meza (Mew / Genesect), 4th Pedro Eugenio Torres (Lost Box Charizard), 5th Aidan Khus (Goodra Lost Zone Box), 6th Trevore Read (Lugia Archeops), 7th Eric Rodriguez (Lugia Archeops), 8th Robert Kinbrum (Miraidon / Regieleki).
- Tord Reklev's 2nd-place Gardevoir ex list played 1 Worker and 1 Gardevoir (Chilling Reign 61).
- Inteleon VMAX / Urshifu decks appeared twice in the published lists, in 11th and 151st.
- The event started on the same day the 2023 Standard rotation took effect for in-person play (14 April 2023), and was one of the first big events with Scarlet & Violet cards legal.
