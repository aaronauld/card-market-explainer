# Sword & Shield Crown Zenith released

## Summary

Crown Zenith, a special expansion of the Sword & Shield series, was released worldwide on 20 January 2023. It is sold in collection products, tins and Elite Trainer Boxes.

## Details

- **Galarian Gallery:** a 70-card subset of special-art Pokémon, Pokémon V, VMAX, VSTAR and Supporter cards, including Mewtwo VSTAR, Darkrai VSTAR and Hisuian Zoroark VSTAR. Nine of the cards join into one larger illustration.
- **Main set:** 159 cards in the main numbering. Cards include Radiant Charizard (Crown Zenith 20), Mew V (60), Zacian V and VSTAR (95, 96), Zamazenta V and VSTAR (98, 99), Regigigas V and VSTAR (113, 114) and Lost Vacuum (135).
- **Launch products:** Elite Trainer Box and Pokémon Center Elite Trainer Box Plus (both with a Lucario VSTAR promo) and a Regieleki V or Regidrago V Collection. Tins, mini tins and premium figure collections (Shiny Zacian, Shiny Zamazenta) followed in later waves.
