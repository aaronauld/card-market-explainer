# Regional Championship Baltimore 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Baltimore Regional Championship, held four weeks after the 2024 World Championships, ran from 13 to 15 September 2024, with 2,307 Masters players. Henry Chao won with a Gardevoir ex deck.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Shrouded Fable (BRS-SFA). Stellar Crown, released on 13 September, was not legal.
- **Top 8:** 1st Henry Chao (Gardevoir), 2nd Ciaran Farah (Lugia / Archeops), 3rd Emmet Hurley (Roaring Moon), 4th Steven Varesko (Lugia / Archeops), 5th Rahul Reddy (Lugia / Archeops), 6th Nolan Randall (Cornerstone Ogerpon / Noivern), 7th Rowan Stavenow (Regidrago), 8th Ian Robb (Regidrago).
- **Lugia VSTAR** (Silver Tempest) decks: five in the top 16 (2nd, 4th, 5th, 11th, 15th).
- **Regidrago VSTAR** (Silver Tempest 136) decks: three in the top 16 (7th, 8th, 16th Charlie Lockyer).
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift): Emmet Hurley's 3rd place was the only Roaring Moon deck in the top 16.
