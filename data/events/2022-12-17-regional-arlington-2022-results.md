# Regional Championship Arlington, Texas 2022, Masters results

## Summary

The Arlington, Texas Regional Championship started on 17 December 2022, with 1,224 Masters players. Connor Finton won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Connor Finton (Lugia Archeops), 2nd Alex Krekeler (Vikavolt), 3rd Jon Eng (Lugia Archeops), 4th Jordon Gaubert (Lugia Archeops), 5th Liam Halliburton (Lugia Archeops), 6th Riley Hulbert (Lugia Archeops), 7th Christian Fontenot (Regis), 8th Jacob Eye (Lost Zone Rayquaza Box).
- Connor Finton's winning Lugia list included 1 Amazing Rare Raikou (Vivid Voltage). About 10 of the 49 published Lugia lists at the event played it, a similar share to the Latin America International Championships a few weeks earlier.
