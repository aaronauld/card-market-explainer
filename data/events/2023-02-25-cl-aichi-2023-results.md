# Champions League Aichi 2023 (Japan), results

## Summary

Champions League Aichi, a Japanese tournament with 3,140 players, was held on 25 February 2023. Shizuya Yamamoto won with Lost Zone Box, and Gardevoir ex decks finished 2nd and 7th.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Shizuya Yamamoto (Lost Zone Box), 2nd Riku Sato (Gardevoir), 3rd Ayato Fudetani (Lost Zone Box), 4th Tomokazu Wakayama (Fusion Mew), 5th Kouga Tsujiguchi (Lugia Archeops), 6th Kazuma Sato (Lugia Archeops), 7th Kohei Otsuki (Gardevoir), 8th Yuto Umemura (Lost Zone Box).
- **Gardevoir ex lists:** Riku Sato's 2nd-place list played 3 Gardevoir (Chilling Reign 61) and 1 Worker (Silver Tempest). Kohei Otsuki's 7th-place list played 2 Gardevoir (Chilling Reign 61) and 1 Worker.
- Gardevoir ex was already legal in Japan at this point. The English version arrived later, in the Scarlet & Violet base set (31 March 2023).
