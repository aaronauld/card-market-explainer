# Japanese Scarlet ex and Violet ex released

## Summary

Scarlet ex (SV1S) and Violet ex (SV1V), the first Japanese expansions of the Scarlet & Violet era, were released in Japan on 20 January 2023.

## Details

- **Scarlet ex** centres on Koraidon ex and also includes Gardevoir ex and Gyarados ex. **Violet ex** centres on Miraidon ex and also includes Arcanine ex. Each set has 78 cards in its main numbering (108 with secret rares).
- These were Japanese releases only. Koraidon ex, Miraidon ex, Gardevoir ex, Gyarados ex and Arcanine ex were released in English in the Scarlet & Violet base set on 31 March 2023.
