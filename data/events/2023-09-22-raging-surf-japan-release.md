# Raging Surf (Japanese SV3a) released in Japan

## Summary

Raging Surf (レイジングサーフ, SV3a), a Japanese Scarlet & Violet sub-set, was released in Japan on 22 September 2023. Its cards were later included in the English Paradox Rift set (3 November 2023).

## Details

- **Size:** 62 numbered cards, 92 including secret rares.
- **Pokémon ex:** Froslass ex, Garchomp ex, Tapu Koko ex, Hoopa ex, Gholdengo ex and Bombirdier ex.
- **Klawf** appears as card 031. A Klawf card is also in the English Paradox Rift set (105).
- Japanese sets are legal only in Japanese tournaments; English players get the cards when the matching English set is released.
