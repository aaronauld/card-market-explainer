# Regional Championship Peoria 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Peoria Regional Championship (Illinois, USA) had 1,703 Masters players, with Masters play starting on 7 October 2023. Raymond Long won with Comfey / Sableye (Lost Zone Box); Jac Carter finished 2nd with the same archetype.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Battle Styles to Obsidian Flames, before Paradox Rift (3 November 2023) was released.
- **Top 8:** 1st Raymond Long (Comfey / Sableye), 2nd Jac Carter (Comfey / Sableye), 3rd Rowan Stavenow (Mew / Genesect), 4th JW Kriewall (Miraidon ex / Flaaffy), 5th Jesse Parker (Miraidon ex / Flaaffy), 6th Terrence Miller (Comfey / Sableye / Charizard ex), 7th Brent Tonisson (Gardevoir ex), 8th Grant Shen (Chien-Pao ex / Baxcalibur).
- Gardevoir ex was one of the most common decks in the standings, with more than 20 Gardevoir players listed; Brent Tonisson's 7th-place list was the best. His list played no Avery (Chilling Reign).
