# Scarlet & Violet—Paldea Evolved released

## Summary

Paldea Evolved, the second main expansion of the Scarlet & Violet series, was released in English on 9 June 2023.

## Details

- **Featured Pokémon ex:** the fully evolved starter Pokémon Meowscarada ex, Skeledirge ex and Quaquaval ex; Chien-Pao ex and Ting-Lu ex; Tera Pokémon ex Forretress, Slowking and Dedenne; and Pikachu ex.
