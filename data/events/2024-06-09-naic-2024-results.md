# North America International Championships 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The North America International Championships (NAIC) 2024 ran in New Orleans, Louisiana, from 7 to 9 June 2024, with about 2,700 Masters players. Andrew Hedrick won with Lost Zone Box, beating Stéphane Ivanoff's Gardevoir ex in the final.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Andrew Hedrick (Lost Zone Box), 2nd Stéphane Ivanoff (Gardevoir), 3rd Keito Arai (Gardevoir), 4th Kaiwen Cabbabe (Gardevoir), 5th Sebastian Lashmet (Gardevoir), 6th Henry Chao (Gardevoir), 7th Natalie Millar (Gardevoir), 8th Andrew Gantner (Lost Zone Box). Gardevoir ex took six of the eight top spots.
- **Regidrago VSTAR** (Silver Tempest) decks: Jacob Eye finished 14th and Ryan Miller 43rd. Both lists played 3 Regidrago VSTAR.
- It was the last International Championships of the 2024 season, held about two weeks after the release of Twilight Masquerade.
