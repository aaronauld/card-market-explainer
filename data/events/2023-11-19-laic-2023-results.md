# Latin America International Championships 2023-24, Masters results

## Summary

The Latin America International Championships (LAIC) for the 2024 season ran in São Paulo, Brazil, from 17 to 19 November 2023, with about 1,260 Masters players. Juho Kallama won with Miraidon ex / Flaaffy. Cards from Paradox Rift (released 3 November 2023) were legal at the event.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Juho Kallama (Miraidon Flaaffy), 2nd Noah Sawyer (Entei / Iron Valiant), 3rd Ciaran Farah (Gardevoir), 4th Gabriel Veloso (Giratina Lost Zone Box), 5th Brennan Kamerman (Lost Box Charizard), 6th Matthew Burris (Gardevoir), 7th Keito Arai (Snorlax Stall), 8th Jeremy Gibson (Gardevoir).
- **Gardevoir ex** was the most common deck among the published lists, with about 30 lists. Most of them played 1-3 copies of the Supporter card Avery (Chilling Reign), including the Gardevoir lists that finished 3rd, 6th and 8th.
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift) decks appeared in 6 published lists, the best finishing 90th (Pedro Bandeira). Every one of them played 4 Dark Patch, and they played 3-4 Energy Switch.
- **Klawf** (Paradox Rift) with Hisuian Electrode V appeared once, in 85th place (Lucas Pedrosa).
