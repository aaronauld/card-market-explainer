# Champions League Kyoto 2022 (Japan), results

## Summary

Champions League Kyoto, a Japanese tournament with 2,843 players, was held on 10 December 2022. Yuta Tagami won with a Regis deck.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Yuta Tagami (Regis), 2nd Minato Watanabe (Lugia Archeops), 3rd Yuta Nakamura (Eternatus / Weezing), 4th Haruki Miwa (Lost Zone Box), 5th Junnosuke Tanaka (Mew / Genesect), 6th Hiroki Kanno (Lost Zone Box), 7th Shunsuke Sasaki (Lugia Archeops), 8th Hyuga Takagi (Lugia Archeops).
