# Champions League Niigata 2023 (Japan), results

## Summary

Champions League 2023 Niigata was held at Toki Messe in Niigata, Japan, on 6-7 May 2023, with the Masters division (2,748 players) played on 6 May. Yoshiyuki Yamaguchi won with a Gardevoir ex deck.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Yoshiyuki Yamaguchi (Gardevoir), 2nd Yuki Sasagawa (Gardevoir), 3rd Ukyo Kurashita (Comfey / Sableye, Lost Zone Box), 4th Tomosu Yamagata (Arceus / Origin Forme Giratina), 5th Shota Hirota (Lugia Archeops), 6th Nobuhiko Shimizu (Gardevoir), 7th Shigeto Fukumoto (Hisuian Goodra / Comfey), 8th Ryosuke Furukawa (Mew / Genesect / Meloetta).
- Gardevoir decks took 5 of the top 16 places (1st, 2nd, 6th, 13th and 14th; 13th was a Gardevoir / Mewtwo variant).
- Origin Forme Giratina / Comfey decks took four places from 10th to 16th.
- No Charizard, Inteleon or Urshifu deck reached the top 16.
