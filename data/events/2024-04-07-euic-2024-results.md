# Europe International Championships 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Europe International Championships (EUIC) 2024 ran in London starting 5 April 2024, with 2,605 Masters players. Tord Reklev won with Charizard ex / Pidgeot ex.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Tord Reklev (Charizard Pidgeot), 2nd Isaiah Bradner (Giratina Lost Zone Box), 3rd Alessandro Cremascoli (Pidgeot Control), 4th William Azevedo (Charizard Pidgeot), 5th Uffe Axelsen (Future Iron Hands), 6th Juho Kallama (Future Iron Hands), 7th Mark Høstrup (Roaring Moon / Dudunsparce), 8th Bert Wolters (Snorlax / Pidgeot).
- A Roaring Moon ex deck finished 7th.
