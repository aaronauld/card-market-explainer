# Pokémon Japan Championships 2023, TCG Masters results

## Summary

The Pokémon Japan Championships 2023 card game division was held at Makuhari Messe (Chiba, Japan) on 10-11 June 2023, with 1,963 Masters players. Nachi Nezu won with Mew VMAX / Genesect V / Meloetta. Top finishers earned places at the 2023 World Championships in Yokohama in August.

## Details

- **Structure:** 10 best-of-one Swiss rounds on day one, 3 best-of-three Swiss rounds on day two, then a best-of-three top 16.
- **Top 8:** 1st Nachi Nezu (Mew / Genesect / Meloetta), 2nd Taisei Kato (Gardevoir), 3rd Shunsuke Sowa (Mew / Genesect), 4th Ayato Fudetani (Origin Forme Giratina / Comfey), 5th Tom Mizutani (Comfey / Sableye), 6th Akihiro Urata (Giratina / Comfey), 7th Yu Shirokuma (Comfey / Sableye), 8th Yuto Takeuchi (Giratina / Comfey).
- **9th-16th** included Yoshiyuki Yamaguchi (Gardevoir, 9th), Yoshiki Kitaie (Miraidon / Flaaffy, 14th) and two Lugia Archeops decks (15th, 16th).
- Gardevoir ex decks placed 2nd and 9th. Comfey / Sableye Lost Zone decks took four top 16 places.
- No Charizard, Inteleon or Urshifu deck reached the top 16.
