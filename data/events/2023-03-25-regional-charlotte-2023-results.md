# Regional Championship Charlotte, North Carolina 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Charlotte, North Carolina Regional Championship started on 25 March 2023, with 1,170 Masters players. Aidan Khus won with Eternatus VMAX / Galarian Weezing.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Aidan Khus (Eternatus Weezing), 2nd Charlie Kerr (Regis), 3rd Justin Bokhari (Lost Zone Box), 4th Noah Peltier (Vikavolt), 5th Rowan Stavenow (Mew / Genesect), 6th Julio Santos De Leon (Lugia Archeops), 7th Michael Battaglia (Lugia Archeops), 8th Jose Marzan (Eternatus Weezing).
- It was one of the last North American Regionals before the Scarlet & Violet base set (31 March 2023) and the 2023 Standard rotation.
