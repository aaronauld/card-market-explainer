# Stellar Miracle released in Japan

## Summary

Stellar Miracle, a Japanese Scarlet & Violet expansion led by Terapagos ex, was released in Japan on 19 July 2024. Its cards were later released in English in Stellar Crown (13 September 2024).

## Details

- **Size:** 135 cards (102 regular and 33 secret rares).
- **Pokémon ex:** Hydrapple ex, Cinderace ex, Lapras ex, Galvantula ex, Dachsbun ex, Medicham ex, Orthworm ex and Terapagos ex (88).
- **Trainers:** the Supporters Crispin and Lacey, and the Stadium Area Zero Underdepths.
- The set also has Hoothoot and Noctowl, Fan Rotom, Bouffalant and the ACE SPEC Glistening Crystal.
- Sources differ by a day: PokeGuardian gives 19 July, Serebii 20 July.
