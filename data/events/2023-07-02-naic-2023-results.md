# North America International Championships 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The North America International Championships (NAIC) 2023 ran in Columbus, Ohio, from 30 June to 2 July 2023, with 1,897 Masters players. Cyrus Davis of Canada won with an Inteleon VMAX / Rapid Strike Urshifu VMAX deck.

## Details

- **Winning list (Cyrus Davis, Inteleon / Urshifu):** 3 Inteleon V and 3 Inteleon VMAX (both Fusion Strike), 2 Rapid Strike Urshifu V and 2 Rapid Strike Urshifu VMAX (Battle Styles), 2 Remoraid and 2 Octillery, plus Trainers such as 3 Iono and 3 Forest Seal Stone. Few other players at the event used an Inteleon deck.
- **Top 8:** 1st Cyrus Davis (Inteleon Urshifu), 2nd Ian Robb (Arceus / Duraludon / Umbreon), 3rd Calvin Connor (Gardevoir), 4th Rowan Stavenow (Gardevoir), 5th Victor Aung (Lost Zone Box), 6th Michael Catron (Arceus / Umbreon), 7th Christian LaBella (Arceus / Duraludon / Umbreon), 8th Justin Brothers (Lugia Archeops).
- It was the last International Championships before the 2023 World Championships.
