# Regional Championship Vancouver, British Columbia 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Vancouver, British Columbia Regional Championship ran from 10 to 12 March 2023, with 526 Masters players. Ian Robb won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops, and Lugia Archeops filled six of the eight top spots.

## Details

- **Top 8:** 1st Ian Robb (Lugia Archeops), 2nd Cobi Kawasaki (Hisuian Goodra / Comfey), 3rd Zach Lesage (Arceus / Duraludon VMAX), 4th Daniel Zhou (Lugia Archeops), 5th Stefan Tabaco (Lugia Archeops), 6th Corey Godfrey (Lugia Archeops), 7th Dalten Cross (Lugia Archeops), 8th Ciaran Farah (Lugia Archeops).
- **Format:** Standard, with Silver Tempest as the newest legal set. It was played before the 2023 Standard rotation and before the Scarlet & Violet base set was legal.
