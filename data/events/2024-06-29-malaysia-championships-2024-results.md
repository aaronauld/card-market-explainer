# Malaysia Championships 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Malaysia Championships 2024, dated 29 June 2024 by Limitless TCG, had 702 Masters players. Yoong Kai Jian won with a Chien-Pao ex / Baxcalibur deck.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Twilight Masquerade (BRS-TWM).
- **Top 8:** 1st Yoong Kai Jian (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 2nd Nick Kee (Comfey / Sableye), 3rd Jason Wee Chee Siang (Chien-Pao / Baxcalibur), 4th Weera Soon (Regidrago), 5th Joshah Tio Yong Zheng (Raging Bolt / Ogerpon), 6th Brian Geikie (Origin Forme Giratina / Comfey), 7th Yst89 (Lugia / Archeops), 8th Low Kien Xun (Comfey / Sableye).
- **Regidrago VSTAR** (Silver Tempest 136): one Regidrago deck reached the top 8, in 4th place.
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift): a Roaring Moon ex / Flutter Mane deck finished 10th.
- All top 16 players were from Malaysia.
