# Sword & Shield Silver Tempest released

## Summary

Silver Tempest, an expansion of the Sword & Shield series, was released in English on 11 November 2022. Its headline card is Lugia VSTAR.

## Details

- **Featured cards:** Pokémon VSTAR of Lugia, Alolan Vulpix, Serperior, Unown, Mawile and Regidrago, and Regieleki VMAX. The set also has 15 Pokémon V, 3 new Radiant Pokémon and a 30-card Trainer Gallery subset.
- **Size:** 195 cards in the main numbering, 215 counting secret rares.
- **Card numbers:** Lugia V (Silver Tempest 138), Lugia VSTAR (139, alternates 202 and 211), Archeops (147), Regieleki V (57), Regieleki VMAX (58, alternate 198), Regidrago VSTAR (136), Alolan Vulpix VSTAR (34) and the Supporter Worker (167, alternates 195 and 209).
