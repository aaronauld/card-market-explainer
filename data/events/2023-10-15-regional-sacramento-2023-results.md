# Regional Championship Sacramento 2023, Masters results

## Summary

The Sacramento Regional Championship (California, USA) had 1,427 Masters players, with Masters play starting on 14 October 2023. Alexander Flatos won with Lugia VSTAR / Archeops.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Battle Styles to Obsidian Flames. Paradox Rift cards such as Roaring Moon ex and Klawf were not yet legal.
- **Top 8:** 1st Alexander Flatos (Lugia / Archeops), 2nd Niccolo Abate (Arceus VSTAR), 3rd Maia Melban (Comfey / Sableye), 4th Dorian Stotts (Lugia / Archeops), 5th Ryan Harris (Gardevoir ex), 6th Charlie Kerr (Lugia / Archeops), 7th Azul Garcia Griego (Comfey / Sableye), 8th Dominic Viola (Lugia / Archeops).
- Four of the top 8 were Lugia / Archeops decks.
- Gardevoir ex had 16 players in the published standings, the best being Ryan Harris in 5th. His list did not include Avery (Chilling Reign).
