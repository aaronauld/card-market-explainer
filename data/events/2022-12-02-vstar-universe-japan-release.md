# Japanese VSTAR Universe high class pack released

## Summary

VSTAR Universe (S12a), a Japanese high class pack, was released in Japan on 2 December 2022. It collects cards from the Sword & Shield era, many with new artwork.

## Details

- **Product:** boxes of 10 packs with 10 cards each, and every pack guaranteed to contain a Pokémon V, VMAX or VSTAR.
- **Size:** 172 cards in the main numbering, plus secret rares.
- **New rarities:** the set introduced Art Rare (AR) and Special Art Rare (SAR) cards. Early reveals included AR cards of Manaphy, Mew, Diancie, Absol, Hisuian Goodra and Altaria, and SAR cards of Mewtwo VSTAR, Charizard VSTAR, Hisuian Samurott VSTAR and Hisuian Zoroark VSTAR.
- This was a Japanese product, not an English release.
