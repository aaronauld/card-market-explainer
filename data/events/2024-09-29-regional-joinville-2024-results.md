# Regional Championship Joinville 2024, Masters results

## Summary

The Joinville Regional Championship in Brazil ran on 28 and 29 September 2024 at Expoville, with 578 Masters players. Yerco Valencia of Chile won with a Lugia VSTAR / Archeops deck.

## Details

- **Format:** Standard, Brilliant Stars to Stellar Crown (BRS-SCR).
- **Top 8:** 1st Yerco Valencia (Lugia / Archeops), 2nd Giovanny Sasso (Terapagos / Noctowl), 3rd Alejandro Luciano Eijo (Klawf), 4th Johana Requena (Terapagos / Noctowl), 5th Matias Matricardi (Iron Thorns), 6th Carlos Medina Astete (Snorlax), 7th Rafael Yuiti (Charizard / Pidgeot), 8th Antonio Jardim (Raging Bolt / Ogerpon).
- **Terapagos ex** (Stellar Crown) decks: three in the top 16 (2nd, 4th, 9th).
- **Regidrago VSTAR** (Silver Tempest 136) decks: 13th Gustavo Wada and 14th Laura Caporali Braga.
- **Roaring Moon ex** (Paradox Rift): Andres Lostaunau finished 11th.
