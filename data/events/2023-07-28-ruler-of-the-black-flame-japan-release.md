# Ruler of the Black Flame (Japanese SV3) released in Japan

## Summary

Ruler of the Black Flame, a Japanese Scarlet & Violet expansion (SV3), was released in Japan on 28 July 2023. Its headline card is a Darkness-type Tera Charizard ex.

## Details

- **Size:** 108 cards before secret rares; boxes of 30 packs at 180 yen per pack.
- **Featured cards:** Charizard ex (a Tera Pokémon whose type is Darkness, shown on the box and pack art), Tyranitar ex, Eiscue ex, Pidgeot ex, Revavroom ex and Glimmora ex, and the Supporter Poppy.
- **Related product:** a Ruler of the Black Flame Deck Build Box (4,200 yen) came out the same day, with a 170-card build set and 10 booster packs.
- This was a Japanese release only. The English set Obsidian Flames, which also features Charizard ex, Tyranitar ex and Eiscue ex, came out two weeks later on 11 August 2023.
