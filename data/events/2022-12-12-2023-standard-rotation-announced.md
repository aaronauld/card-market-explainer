# 2023 Standard format rotation announced (D regulation mark leaves)

## Summary

On 12 December 2022, The Pokémon Company International announced the 2023 Standard format rotation: cards with the D regulation mark would no longer be legal in Standard. The change took effect on 30 March 2023 in Pokémon TCG Live and on 14 April 2023 for in-person Play! Pokémon events.

## Details

- **Leaving Standard:** every card with the D regulation mark, whatever set it was printed in.
- **Staying legal:** cards with the E and F regulation marks, and future marks.
- **Effective dates:** 30 March 2023 (Pokémon TCG Live), 14 April 2023 (in-person tournaments).
- **Examples of D-mark cards leaving Standard** (marks from the TCGdex card database): Scoop Up Net (Rebel Clash), Aurora Energy (Sword & Shield), Capture Energy (Rebel Clash), Twin Energy (Rebel Clash) and Training Court (Rebel Clash). Legality depends on each card's mark, not on its set, so a set can contain both D and E cards.
- The same announcement covered an errata making Pokémon Tools their own type of Trainer card from the Scarlet & Violet base set onwards.
