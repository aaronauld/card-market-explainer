# Scarlet & Violet—Twilight Masquerade released

## Summary

Twilight Masquerade, the sixth main expansion of the Scarlet & Violet series, was released in English on 24 May 2024. The Japanese version came out on 26 April 2024.

## Details

- The Pokémon Company International announced the launch date on 14 March 2024.
- Prerelease events started on 11 May 2024, and the set arrived in Pokémon TCG Live on 23 May 2024.
